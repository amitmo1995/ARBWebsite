2. What Information We Collect

The Website is currently a pre-launch informational site. We collect only the following limited categories of information.

a. Email Address — Launch List

If you choose to submit your email address via our "Join the Launch" form, we collect your email address for the purpose described in Section 3. Submission is entirely voluntary. You are not required to provide your email address to browse the Website. Before your submission is accepted, you must actively check a consent checkbox confirming that you agree to this use.

b. Technical / Access Data — Hosting Provider

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider, GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), may automatically collect standard web-server log data. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and the date and time of your request. This data is processed by GitHub in accordance with GitHub's General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or have direct access to these logs.

c. Data Sent to Google — Fonts

Our Website loads typefaces (fonts) from Google's servers (Google Fonts). Each time a page loads, your browser makes a request to Google's servers, and Google will receive your IP address and standard browser information as part of that HTTP request. We have no access to or control over this data. It is processed by Google LLC in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

d. What We Do NOT Collect

We want to be transparent. The following data is not collected by or on behalf of AURBON through this Website:

  • No tracking cookies or advertising cookies are set by us
  • No analytics tools are in use (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no TikTok Pixel, no similar service)
  • No session recording, heatmapping, or behaviour-tracking tools
  • No payment information (the Website does not currently support purchases)
  • No names, phone numbers, or postal addresses beyond what is listed above
  • No user accounts are maintained

3. How and Why We Use Your Information

Launch List Email Addresses

Purpose: To contact you when the AURBON product collection officially launches — including notification of the launch date, product availability, and any exclusive early-access information we choose to share with list members.

Legal basis: Your freely given, specific, and informed consent, provided by you when you check the consent checkbox and submit the launch list form. This basis is consistent with Section 11 of the Privacy Law (as amended).

What we will not do: We will not sell your email address to any third party, share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes, or use it for any purpose other than those explicitly described above. We will not send you unsolicited commercial communications beyond launch-related updates unless you separately consent to further marketing.

Technical / Access Data

Processing of technical access data by GitHub Pages is carried out for the purpose of serving the Website and maintaining its security and stability. We do not use this data to identify individual users and do not receive it directly.

Google Fonts Data

Google receives your IP address as a technical by-product of delivering font files. We use Google Fonts solely to display typefaces on the Website. We do not receive or use any data from this interaction. See Section 6 regarding international transfers.

7. Your Rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (as amended by Amendment No. 13), you have the following rights with respect to personal information we hold about you:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information from our records, subject to any overriding legal obligation to retain it.
  • Right to Object: You may object to certain processing of your personal information.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. To withdraw consent for the launch list, simply contact us at the address below.

To exercise any of these rights, please send a written request to:
office@aurbon-sports.com

We will acknowledge and respond to your request within a reasonable time, and in any event within the timeframes required by applicable Israeli law. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority (Reshut Hagana Al Hapratiyut).
