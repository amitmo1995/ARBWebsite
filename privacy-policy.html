This Privacy Policy describes how AURBON Sports ("AURBON", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information relating to visitors to our website at aurbon-sports.com (the "Website").

We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 ("Privacy Law"), including Amendment No. 13 (which entered into force on August 14, 2023) and any regulations thereunder. Where applicable, we also take into account the guidelines of the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority (Reshut Hagana Al Hapratiyut).

Please read this Policy carefully. By using the Website you acknowledge that you have read and understood it.

1. Who We Are

Data Controller / Database Owner:

AURBON Sports
Address: Jerusalem, Israel
E-mail: office@aurbon-sports.com

For any privacy-related enquiry or to exercise your rights, please contact us at the address above.

2. What Information We Collect

The Website is a pre-launch informational site. We do not directly collect any personal information from visitors.

Technical / Access Data — Hosting Provider

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider, GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), may automatically collect standard web-server log data as part of normal hosting operations. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the date and time of your request. This data is processed by GitHub — not by us — in accordance with GitHub's General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or have direct access to these logs.

Data Sent to Google — Fonts

Our Website loads typefaces from Google's servers (Google Fonts). Each time a page loads, your browser makes a request to Google's servers, and Google will receive your IP address and standard browser information as part of that HTTP request. We have no access to or control over this data. It is processed by Google LLC in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Contact by Email

The Website provides a link to contact us at office@aurbon-sports.com. If you choose to send us an email, your email address and the contents of your message will be received by us. We will use this information only to respond to your enquiry and will not retain it beyond what is necessary for that purpose.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No tracking cookies or advertising cookies are set by us
  • No analytics tools (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no TikTok Pixel, no similar service)
  • No session recording, heatmapping, or behaviour-tracking tools
  • No payment information (the Website does not support purchases)
  • No user accounts
  • No email addresses are collected or stored through any form on the Website

3. How and Why We Use Your Information

Because we do not directly collect personal information through the Website, there is no personal data processed by us beyond:

  • Email enquiries: If you email us, we use your email address and message solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Hosting and fonts: Technical data is processed by GitHub and Google as described above, for the purpose of operating and displaying the Website.

4. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own systems. Email enquiries you send to office@aurbon-sports.com will be retained only for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry, after which they will be deleted.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as follows:

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or binding direction of a competent Israeli authority, or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights or the safety of individuals.

Business Transfer

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of our business assets, personal information we hold may be transferred to the acquiring entity as part of that transaction, subject to the same protections as set out in this Policy.

6. International Data Transfer

The Website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (United States) and loads fonts from Google LLC (United States). Your IP address and browser information may therefore be processed outside of Israel, including in the United States.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that any international transfer of personal information complies with applicable Israeli privacy law. GitHub and Google each maintain Privacy Shield or equivalent transfer frameworks and data-processing commitments. For details, see GitHub's Privacy Statement and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Your Rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (as amended by Amendment No. 13), you have the following rights with respect to personal information we hold about you:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information from our records, subject to any overriding legal obligation to retain it.
  • Right to Object: You may object to certain processing of your personal information.
  • Right to Request Deletion: You may request deletion of any personal information we hold about you (e.g. an email exchange) by contacting us at the address below.

To exercise any of these rights, please send a written request to:
office@aurbon-sports.com

We will acknowledge and respond to your request within a reasonable time, and in any event within the timeframes required by applicable Israeli law. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority (Reshut Hagana Al Hapratiyut).

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information we hold against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of data transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Children

This Website is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under your care has submitted personal information to us without your consent, please contact us at office@aurbon-sports.com and we will take prompt steps to delete such information.

10. Third-Party Links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites, including our Instagram account (@aurbonsports). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or the Website's functionality. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically. Continued use of the Website after an updated Policy is posted constitutes your acceptance of the changes.
